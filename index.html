Co-op Canvas Puzzle

YOYO MAZE

Two players share one Yo-Yo. Player 1 controls its swing direction, while Player 2 controls how much string is let out.

Work together: reach FINISH without hitting walls or holes.

Player 1: Direction

A rotate left

D rotate right

Player 2: String Length

K reel in

L let out

Shared Goal

Avoid dark maze walls and blue holes.

Press R to restart any time.
